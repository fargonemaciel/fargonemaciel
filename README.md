Hi, I'm **Fargone Maciel** 👋

I build machine learning models and AI agents that have to work inside a real operation: national scale, tight deadlines and teams that rely on the output the very next day.

I'm a data scientist and ML engineer at the Data Intelligence division of **Correios**, Brazil's national postal and logistics company. On the company's AWS Big Data platform, I take solutions from experiment to production: predictive and classification models, feature pipelines in Spark and generative AI agents on Bedrock. Before working with data, I spent years inside the logistics operation itself, so I start from the business problem, not the algorithm.

**Highlights**
- 🧠 Led the development of a dangerous-goods classification model that analyzes millions of customer content declarations per month for automated cargo screening
- 🛰️ Coordinated the logistics Control Tower for COP30, with towers in Brasília and Belém, on AWS
- 🔬 Modeled the BigQuery datasets for the data science partnership between Correios and UNICAMP
- 📊 Built Correios' first public dashboard together with Brazil's Ministry of Communications

**Current focus:** RAG, AI agents in production and MLOps on AWS (Bedrock, EMR, Lambda, Airflow).

**🚧 Coming soon:** cloud-native ML and generative AI projects on AWS will be published here.
